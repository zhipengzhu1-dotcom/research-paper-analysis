3 Background
Recently, there has been an explosion of open-weight LLMs with near-frontier capabilities (Qwen Team, 2026; GLM-5-
Team et al., 2026; Kimi Team, T. Bai, Y. Bai, Bao, C, et al., 2026). Using these models as starting points, we consider the
setting of post-training a capable open-weight language model to succeed at molecular design tasks, including
those with high evaluation cost. Although several post-training methods (e.g., supervised finetuning (Ouyang et al.,
2022; Wei, Bosma, et al., 2022), on-policy context distillation (T. Ye et al., 2026), DPO (Rafailov et al., 2024), PPO (Schulman
et al., 2017)) are used to augment capabilities of models, in this work, we restrict ourselves to a relatively simple
post-training approach: reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) (Lambert et al., 2025; Shao et al., 2024).
