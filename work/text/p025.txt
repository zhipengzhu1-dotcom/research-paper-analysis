C.3 Analysis of model submissions
For our evaluation setting, the model is tasked with returning its final proposed molecule enclosed between the
<|answer_start|> and <|answer_end|> tags, an instruction that is provided in its task prompt (see Prompts 2
and 3). However, we observe that in certain cases, the model does not return its best scored molecule, instead
returning an alternative design that scores worse. We quantify this across all models for the w obj = 0.75, w constraint =
0.25 condition in Figure A5 and carry out analysis only on rollouts that call score_sqm. By restricting analysis to
these rollouts, we can assess whether models that get feedback from the scoring tool actually pick the best molecule
that they scored or instead demonstrate alternate behavior.
